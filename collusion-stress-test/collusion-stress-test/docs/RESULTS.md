# Simulation results (engine v0.2.0)

Generated by `node scripts/study.mjs`. **These are results demonstrated by our simulations** in one stylized market (symmetric logit-demand Bertrand, Calvano et al. 2020 design); they are not evidence about any real firm and not legal conclusions. Δ = 0 is the competitive (Nash) benchmark, Δ = 1 the joint-profit maximum. Tables show mean [95% bootstrap CI over seeds]. Baseline = noisy myopic best-responders.

## Limits

- Single market family; symmetric firms; fixed run length (200k periods is far shorter than the multi-million-period runs in the literature, so results may understate or differ from converged behavior).
- With 3 firms the state space is 15³ = 3,375 states; 200k periods gives each state far fewer visits, so n=3 results reflect less-trained agents.
- Findings thresholds are screening parameters, not calibrated error rates.
