# Sources

Bibliographic details were written from knowledge of the literature and should be verified (especially pages) before external citation.

- Calvano, Calzolari, Denicolò, Pastorello (2020). "Artificial Intelligence, Algorithmic Pricing, and Collusion." *American Economic Review* 110(10), 3267–3297. Basis for the market, Q-learning design, Δ metric, and deviation test.
- Klein (2021). "Autonomous algorithmic collusion: Q-learning under sequential pricing." *RAND Journal of Economics* 52(3).
- Asker, Fershtman, Pakes (2022). "Artificial Intelligence, Algorithm Design, and Pricing." *AEA Papers and Proceedings* 112. Algorithm design affects outcomes.
- Assad, Clark, Ershov, Xu (2024). "Algorithmic Pricing and Competition: Empirical Evidence from the German Retail Gasoline Market." *Journal of Political Economy* 132(3). Empirical evidence.
- Fish, Gonczarowski, Shorrer (2024). "Algorithmic Collusion by Large Language Models." arXiv:2404.00806. Motivates future LLM-agent support.
- Green, Porter (1984). "Noncooperative Collusion under Imperfect Price Information." *Econometrica* 52(1). Motivates the deferred noisy-demand condition.
- Harrington (2018). "Developing Competition Law for Collusion by Autonomous Artificial Agents." *Journal of Competition Law & Economics* 14(3).
- OECD (2017). *Algorithms and Collusion: Competition Policy in the Digital Age.*
