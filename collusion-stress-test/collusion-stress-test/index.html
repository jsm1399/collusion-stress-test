⚖️ Collusion Stress-Test

Open, reproducible testing of autonomous pricing agents in simulated markets. Runs entirely in your browser.
Read results carefully. A simulation can show behavior that deserves investigation. It cannot prove an agreement, intent, or a violation of antitrust law.
Advanced: full experiment configuration (JSON)
Parameter sweep Repeats the whole experiment above for several values of one setting to see how behavior changes.
Tip: lower the seeds or periods above first; a sweep runs the full experiment once per value.
Standard test suite (CST-1 v1.0.0) A fixed battery of 7 market scenarios (close/distant substitutes, three firms, noisy demand, noisy observation, competitive rival) applied identically to any agent, so results are comparable.
Q-learning at the defaults takes several minutes.
